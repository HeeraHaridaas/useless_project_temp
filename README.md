### Project Description
[Kerala Kadi Tinder is a satirical Tinder-style food matchmaking app where users choose a dish identity and swipe through potential culinary relationships. The app calculates compatibility, generates hilarious dish-specific pairing explanations, reveals official and unconventional matches, and produces a final Food Bureau verdict. It is part dating simulator, part food gossip network, and entirely unnecessary.
]

### The Problem (that doesn't exist)
[The world has apps for dating people, finding restaurants, ordering food, tracking calories, and discovering recipes. But there is still no proper system to answer the most urgent question of our generation:

“If I were a food, what food would I be compatible with?”

People regularly make chaotic food combinations without understanding the consequences. Puttu meets kadala curry and nobody questions it, but pizza meets sambar and suddenly society has concerns. Traditional food pairings are accepted without investigation, while unconventional combinations are rejected without giving them a fair chance.

Kerala Kadi Tinder solves this completely unnecessary crisis by creating a matchmaking platform for food.]

### The Solution (that nobody asked for)
[We solve this completely unnecessary food crisis by treating every dish like a potential soulmate, rival, situationship, or questionable life decision.

Kerala Kadi Tinder transforms food discovery into an interactive matchmaking experience. Users can choose the dish that represents them, take a personality quiz to discover their culinary identity, or introduce a new food into the system. They then swipe through a chaotic food universe, judging dishes based on flavour, texture, spice, sweetness, eating habits, cultural compatibility, and overall plate behaviour.

A right swipe triggers a dramatic Food Bureau investigation. The system determines whether the pairing is a legendary combination, a complicated arrangement, an obvious food crime, or an unexpectedly brilliant disaster. Every result includes unique pairing reasons, relationship statuses, food gossip, and sarcastic verdicts.

If users disagree with the official decision, they can challenge the Bureau and approve unconventional pairings because sometimes tradition is wrong and chaos deserves representation.

Through personalised food profiles, humorous compatibility reports, dramatic match reveals, and a final culinary verdict, we turn an ordinary food choice into a ridiculous relationship adventure.

**We are not just helping people find what to eat. We are helping food discover who it belongs with, who it should avoid, and which combinations require immediate government investigation.**
]

### Implementation
For Software:[The application was developed as an interactive web application using React and JavaScript through Freebuff.

The implementation includes:

A Tinder-style swipe interface for food discovery
Food identity selection
Food personality quiz
User-added food registration
AI-generated food descriptions and profiles
Deterministic food compatibility scoring
Pair-specific match explanations
Official and unconventional match options
Food Bureau verdicts
Match confirmation and session tracking
Final personalized food relationship report
Responsive design for desktop and mobile devices

The compatibility score is calculated locally using predefined food characteristics and pairing rules. AI is used only for creative content such as descriptions, jokes, pairing reasons, and verdicts. This ensures that the actual matching logic remains consistent and reliable.]
